SUPPORT

Start with the connection.

Open Pocket Daily on the compatible reader, choose Nearby Sync, then choose Find & Connect in the Apple-platform app.

Reading books

Add DRM-free EPUB, TXT or Markdown files with + in the Library. Tap the sides of the page to turn, the middle for Contents and text settings. Books bought with DRM cannot be opened.

Continue reading

Your place follows you through iCloud on devices signed in to the same Apple ID, and is exchanged with your X3/X4 reader while it is connected. Turn either off in Library → Continue Reading.

No reader found

Keep both devices awake and nearby. Confirm Bluetooth and Local Network access, or use the reader's Create Hotspot fallback.

Try the interface

Open Reader and choose Try demo. Demo mode is local and disables transfer and device changes.

Update the reader

Connect over the same Wi-Fi and tap Update reader in the Reader panel. Pocket Daily downloads the latest official firmware release, checks it and sends it; the reader asks before installing when you leave its transfer screen. A reader still on factory firmware needs a one-time USB install (see the firmware README).

Firmware safety

Use only firmware built for the exact hardware profile. Pocket Daily stages the file; installation is confirmed separately on the reader.

Report a problem

Export the in-app connection log or crash report, remove secrets, and attach it to a GitHub issue.

Scope. Support covers Pocket Daily and compatible CrossPoint-based firmware, not factory firmware, manufacturer cloud services, hardware warranty, or physical repair.
