SUPPORT

Read here. Continue on your reader.

Your Library works without a reader or an account. When you want to send a book or customize a compatible reader, Pocket Daily helps you connect from that task.

Reading books

Add DRM-free EPUB, TXT or Markdown files with + in the Library. Tap the sides of the page to turn, the middle for Contents and text settings. Books bought with DRM cannot be opened.

Articles and subscriptions

Open Library → Articles → + → Subscriptions and paste a direct HTTPS RSS or Atom feed URL. New articles refresh when you open the app or choose Refresh. Tap a saved body to read offline. Use the article menu to save it for later, mark it read or unread, edit its text, or prepare it for your reader. Link-only entries need Get full text or pasted text before reading. Unsubscribing keeps your articles. Email-only newsletters can be added by sharing their web link or text; email accounts and article-library cloud sync are not supported.

Continue reading

Your place can be shared through iCloud on devices signed in to the same Apple ID, and exchanged with your X3/X4 reader while it is connected. You choose before moving to another device's place. Manage either option in Settings → Continue Reading; Bluetooth pairing is in My Reader → Device.

Connect your reader

Choose Find on same Wi-Fi with the reader in its same-network transfer mode. This keeps your internet connection and does not need Bluetooth pairing. If you need a direct connection, open the other connection methods and choose Connect directly. The app explains the temporary Wi-Fi switch before you approve it. Prepare cloud files first. Cancel stops the current attempt; wait for cleanup before retrying.

Send a book

Choose Send to Reader… on a Library book or saved article. The task keeps that selection while you connect; choose Send when ready. On Mac, you can choose an SD card destination in the same task and select its folder. An SD copy is reported separately from a wireless transfer. Close and reopen the task to follow its progress. Other prepared books and firmware are not included. If the save result is uncertain, reconnect the original reader from that task and check it before resending. My Reader → On Reader shows the files last observed on your device.

Arrange your reader screens

Open My Reader → Screens, choose Home or Sleep, adjust its layout and apply that screen. Shared cards and weather/calendar sources open in their own editors with their own apply action. Closing an editor returns to the same screen layout; source edits stay local until applied. Reading controls the reader's book layout, orientation and page-button actions separately. Device holds connections, firmware and diagnostics.

Try the interface

Open My Reader → Device and choose Try demo. Demo mode is local and disables transfer and device changes.

Update the reader

Connect over the same Wi-Fi and tap Update reader in the Device screen. Pocket Daily downloads the latest official firmware release, checks it and sends it; the reader asks before installing when you leave its transfer screen. A reader still on factory firmware needs a one-time USB install (see the firmware README).

Firmware safety

Use only firmware built for the exact hardware profile. Pocket Daily stages the file; installation is confirmed separately on the reader.

Report a problem

Export the in-app connection log or crash report, remove secrets, and attach it to a GitHub issue.

Scope. Support covers Pocket Daily and compatible CrossPoint-based firmware, not factory firmware, manufacturer cloud services, hardware warranty, or physical repair.
