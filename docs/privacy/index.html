PRIVACY POLICY · AUGUST 31, 2026

Local by design.

Pocket Daily does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising network, or cloud relay. We do not collect data from the app.

Information Pocket Daily handles

The app handles device discovery signals, compatible reader status, reader preferences, user-selected books, study packs, fonts, firmware files, and reader crash diagnostics only to perform actions the user requests. This information stays on the user's Apple device, local network, compatible reader, SD card, or another location explicitly selected by the user.

Bluetooth, local network, and Wi-Fi permission

Bluetooth is used to discover and pair with a nearby compatible reader. The local network and a temporary reader Wi-Fi network are used for direct transfer. On supported Apple operating systems, system location permission may be required to configure or join Wi-Fi; Pocket Daily does not request or read geographic coordinates.

Files and diagnostics

Files are never uploaded to a Pocket Daily server. Reader crash reports may be archived locally for diagnosis and leave the device only when the user explicitly exports them. Removing the app deletes data held in its app container; files already copied to a reader, SD card, or user-selected folder remain under the user's control.

Third parties and children

Pocket Daily contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information through a developer-operated service.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be published here and reflected in the App Store privacy answers. Questions can be filed through Pocket Daily Support.

Policy source and change history are available in the public Pocket Daily repository.
