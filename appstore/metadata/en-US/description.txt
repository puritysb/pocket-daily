Pocket Daily is a calm, private e-book reader for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and the companion for compatible X3/X4 pocket e-paper readers.

A quiet place to read
Open DRM-free EPUB books in a paper-like reader that stays out of the way. Pages turn instantly, like e-paper. Tap the sides to turn, tap the middle for controls, or use a keyboard or Bluetooth page turner. Choose the text size, font, line spacing, margins, and a Paper, White or Night page. Reading controls stay hidden until you need them.

Your library, your files
Add EPUB books from Files, or drop them on the Mac window. Plain text and Markdown files become books when you add them. Save articles from the share sheet or follow RSS and Atom feeds, including newsletters that offer a feed. Read saved text offline, keep favourites for later, and send articles to your reader when you choose. A short guide is included, so you can start reading right away.

Continue where you left off
Your place follows you through your own iCloud on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, and is exchanged with your X3/X4 reader while it is connected, with no Pocket Daily account or server to set up. When another device read more recently or got further, Pocket Daily offers to jump there; it never moves your page without asking. Share the same book file to each device from the Library.

Companion for your X3/X4 reader
Choose which Home pages the reader shows and in what order, place the weather panel, and compose the Daily Brief it shows while asleep. The preview is drawn by the reader's own layout code. Write up to three cards with images or QR codes. Send books, articles and typed text over a direct local connection, or copy files to an SD card on Mac. Firmware updates show the latest official release and install only after you confirm on the reader.

Private by design
No Pocket Daily account. No ads. No analytics. No cloud relay. Your library stays on your device. Discovery and transfer use Bluetooth and the local network. Weather requests send only the chosen city to Apple Weather. Reading positions stay in your own iCloud and on your reader; the book itself is never shared.

Try the companion before connecting
Demo mode shows the reader editors with labeled sample content and never sends files or changes a device.

Compatibility notice
Reading needs no extra hardware. The companion features need a separately obtained X3/X4-class reader running Pocket Daily or compatible CrossPoint-based firmware; Home and Sleep editing needs Pocket Daily firmware. Factory firmware, manufacturer cloud services and DRM-protected books are not supported. Pocket Daily is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any reader manufacturer, or the CrossPoint Reader project.
