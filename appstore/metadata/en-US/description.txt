Pocket Daily is a private, local-first studio for a compatible pocket e-paper reader.

Shape your reader's Home and Sleep screens
Choose which Home pages appear and in what order (continue reading, study cards, provider cards, a monitoring summary), place the weather panel, and compose the Daily Brief the reader shows while asleep. The preview is drawn by the reader's own layout code with sample content, and one Send saves the layout on the reader without reinstalling firmware.

Write study cards that look right on e-paper
Edit up to three cards with text and images and see each one at the reader's resolution before you send it. Drafts are saved on your device, and each Send refreshes the reader over the same connection.

Move your own content directly
Send supported books, Pocket Daily study packs, and compatible firmware over a direct local connection. On Mac, you can also copy files to an SD card. Transfers do not pass through a Pocket Daily cloud service.

Safer device management
Adjust reader settings, and stage firmware only after a clear compatibility warning. Firmware installation still requires confirmation on the reader.

Private by design
No Pocket Daily account. No ads. No analytics. No cloud relay. Discovery and transfer use Bluetooth and the local network. Files stay between your Apple device, your reader, and storage you choose.

Try before connecting
Built-in demo mode shows the editors with labeled sample content and no reader hardware. Demo mode never sends files or changes a device.

Compatibility notice
A separately obtained X3/X4-class reader running Pocket Daily or compatible CrossPoint-based firmware is required for live transfer and settings; Home and Sleep editing needs Pocket Daily firmware that stores these settings. Factory firmware and manufacturer cloud services are not supported. Pocket Daily is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any reader manufacturer or the CrossPoint Reader project.
