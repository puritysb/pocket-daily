Pocket Daily is a private, local-first studio for a compatible pocket e-paper reader.

Shape your reader's Home and Sleep screens
Choose which Home pages appear and in what order (continue reading, your own cards, the daily word), place the weather panel, and compose the Daily Brief the reader shows while asleep. Weather for a city you choose comes from Apple Weather, and today's events can come from your calendars; the app sends both to the reader. The preview is drawn by the reader's own layout code with your cards and sample content, and one Send saves the layout and reader settings without reinstalling firmware.

Your own cards, with QR codes
Write up to three cards (a reading goal, a question, contact details in case the reader is lost) and add an image, a QR code made from a link or text, or an image from a link. The first card can stay on the sleep screen. If you lose your local copy, load the cards back from the reader.

Send what you want to read
Send books (EPUB, TXT, Markdown, XTC), Pocket Daily study packs, or text you type or paste, over a direct local connection. On Mac, you can also copy files to an SD card. Transfers do not pass through a Pocket Daily cloud service.

Safer device management
Stage firmware only after a clear compatibility warning. Firmware installation still requires confirmation on the reader.

Private by design
No Pocket Daily account. No ads. No analytics. No cloud relay. Discovery and transfer use Bluetooth and the local network. Files and calendar events stay between your Apple device, your reader, and storage you choose; weather requests send only the chosen city to Apple Weather.

Try before connecting
Built-in demo mode shows the editors with labeled sample content and no reader hardware. Demo mode never sends files or changes a device.

Compatibility notice
A separately obtained X3/X4-class reader running Pocket Daily or compatible CrossPoint-based firmware is required for live transfer and settings; Home and Sleep editing needs Pocket Daily firmware that stores these settings. Factory firmware and manufacturer cloud services are not supported. Pocket Daily is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any reader manufacturer or the CrossPoint Reader project.
