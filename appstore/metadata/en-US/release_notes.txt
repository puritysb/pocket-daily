Pocket Daily 1.0 is a calm e-book reader for iPhone, iPad and Mac and the companion for X3/X4 readers: a paper-like reader for DRM-free EPUB, text and Markdown, a library with offline articles and RSS/Atom subscriptions, continue reading through iCloud and the reader, a Home and Sleep editor previewed with the reader's own layout code, cards with QR codes, direct transfers, SD-card copy on Mac, and guarded firmware updates.
