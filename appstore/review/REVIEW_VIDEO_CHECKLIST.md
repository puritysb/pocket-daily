# Physical-reader review video checklist

Record one continuous 60–90 second video with the iPhone/iPad or Mac screen and the physical reader visible together.

1. Show the reader model and Pocket Daily firmware About/version screen.
2. Enter Nearby Sync on the reader and show that a pairing code appears, while keeping the actual characters unreadable (camera angle, crop, or blur).
3. Tap Find & Connect in the app; show successful pairing and the connected device/version row.
4. Send the included original `Pocket-Daily-Review-Sample.epub` so the reviewer can identify the exact transferred content without a copyright or privacy concern.
5. Show transfer progress completing in the app and the content appearing on the reader.
6. Open reader settings from the app, change one harmless setting, apply it, and show the result on the reader.
7. Select a current Pocket Daily firmware image, show the safety confirmation sheet, and show that local validation begins; do not install firmware during the review video.
8. End on About & Privacy, showing the independent-project and local-first notices.

Do not expose a home Wi-Fi password, Bluetooth pairing secret, personal book library, device serial number, email address, or notification content. Upload the finished video as unlisted and paste its URL only in App Store Connect review notes.
